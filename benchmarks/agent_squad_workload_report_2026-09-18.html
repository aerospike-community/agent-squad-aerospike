Every scheduled call completed successfully on both backends. The benchmark runner now clamps early-completion response samples to zero rather than dropping them. Aerospike kept response p99 below 5.6 ms at 100 QPS and below 4.4 ms at 500 QPS across all scenarios.

Method and environment

Both runs used identical dimensions: 256-byte payloads, 10-message retained histories, four agents per session, a 1,024-key read pool, 256 maximum in-flight operations, one-second warmup, 30 seconds per function, and deterministic seed 20260915. Each backend/function pair received 3,000 calls at 100 QPS and 15,000 calls at 500 QPS. Response latency includes scheduler queueing; service latency measures execution only.

Interpretation

  • Both backends completed all 216,000 scheduled calls without operational failures.
  • At 100 QPS, Aerospike had lower service and response latency at every reported percentile in all scenarios.
  • At 500 QPS, Aerospike had lower response latency at every reported percentile in all scenarios.
  • DynamoDB Local completed all scheduled calls but accumulated substantial response queueing at 500 QPS, especially on the contended path; low execution-only service latency does not imply low end-to-end latency.
  • The two adapters use different persistence paths, and the local DynamoDB emulator is not representative of managed DynamoDB.
